Codebase Context

PortSpenderNote runs as a local-first Next.js 16 + Go API stack (app/api/**/route.go delegating domain logic to internal/), backed by a single-writer SQLite database (data/spender.db, STRICT tables with inline CHECK constraints in WAL mode) and a local OpenAI-compatible 7B inference server (http://localhost:9931/v1, currently itlwas/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct-Q4_K_M-GGUF:Q4_K_M, Temperature: 0, CachePrompt: true in internal/llm/client.go:244).

  • 1 of 6 (4f1ddad2, Shopee security alert) is a true negative with amount_decimal: null.
  • 4 dismissed rows expose real false positives: cancelled Jenius charge 1e4bfdde + cancellation fb13b866, Grab voucher 16006a26, and billing statement 0b809370.

Root Cause of the 5 Skipped Expenses: Inspecting email_extractions.raw_llm_json in data/spender.db confirms that the local LLM already parsed valid amounts and currencies (and accounts on 4 of 5) for every reported email before emitting email_kind: "non_transaction":

  • 5ac46a86-0d1e-4f29-99ef-cc8f9882cace — bca@bca.co.id, QRIS Payment to Ayam Keprabon Express (IDR 34,500, 5260****55).
  • eb26eaca-ce81-4f8d-bedc-f13d4fa3456d — bca@bca.co.id, QRIS Payment to TIONG INDOGROSIR -HO (IDR 93,500).
  • 9e83e37c-d03a-4ff2-a1d3-eb3bed1202ed — jenius_noreply@smbci.com, Jenius transfer to ANKIE ERTMA RAHFREEL (IDR 100,000, extracted on Qwen2.5-3B).
  • f9e81377-d39a-4bec-a3b5-67366146df45 — no-reply@grab.com, Grab Tips E-Receipt for GrabFood (IDR 5,000, card_last4: 5260).

Because NormalizeExtractedResult (internal/llm/normalize.go:276-282) immediately discards every field except RawJSON when raw.EmailKind == "non_transaction", and runExtractionWorker (internal/syncrunner/runner.go:680-696) returns early before checking senders.role_hint, these valid transactions vanish behind a bare Skipped counter in SyncToast with no inspection or recovery UI. Meanwhile, BuildExtractionPrompt (internal/llm/prompt.go:83-86) recognizes only card_charge, receipt, and non_transaction (omitting QRIS, bank transfers, and tips).

Current Learning Blind Spots & Architectural Constraints: The app's sole learning mechanism — findRemarkExamples (internal/domain/enrichment.go:143-170) — filters remark_user_edited = 1 rows via instr(lower(?), lower(prefix)) > 0, which structurally blocks user-invented prefixes like DineOut: from matching BCA QRIS emails. MatchAccountForExtraction (internal/domain/accounts_matching.go:64,71,97) short-circuits past bank_account and alias checks whenever card_last4 collides. Past repo learnings (docs/solutions/) establish four key boundaries: deterministic rules beat single prose prompt bullets on 7B GGUF models; additive SQLite tables avoid multi-process goRoutes STRICT/CHECK rebuild races; local re-evaluation over raw_emails must never mutate Gmail cursors; and core logic belongs in internal/. External prior art (Rossum IDP, SQLite FTS5 BM25 dynamic retrieval, Monarch/Actual/Lunch Money edit-to-rule flywheels, and Cursor Tab "Learning Silence") reinforces pairing deterministic local rules with concise KV-cache-friendly dynamic retrieval.

Topic Axes

  • 1. Triage & Recovery Gate Preventing false non_transaction skips and false-positive imports, surfacing skipped/borderline emails for rescue or rejection, and feeding those verdicts back into future triage.
  • 2. Correction-to-Knowledge Distillation Turning existing ledger edits (email_extractions vs transactions) into durable, inspectable, deterministic personal knowledge rather than leaving them inert in SQLite.
  • 3. Context-Smart Local 7B Prompting Delivering personal history to the local quantized 7B model within tight token and CachePrompt KV-cache budgets across both sync extraction and Magic LLM.
  • 4. Magic LLM Surfaces & Proactive Workflows Expanding Magic LLM beyond manual per-drawer clicks into user-data-aware enrichment, retroactive cleanup, provenance transparency, and calibrated abstention.
  • 5. Cross-Email & Ledger Relationship Learning Resolving colliding account identities and learning multi-email lifecycle relationships (card charge + receipt, tip add-ons, charge + cancellation pairs).

1. Contradiction-Aware Truth-Ledger Triage & Inline Timeline Ghost Rows

Triage & Recovery Gate Confidence 92% (High) Complexity Medium
Description
Stop discarding parsed transaction fields when the 7B model emits email_kind: "non_transaction" despite extracting a positive amount_decimal and currency. Decouple observable money-flow labeling (5 tractable classes suited to a quantized 7B model: purchase_or_qris, bank_transfer, tip_addon, cancellation_or_refund, non_spend_notice) from a deterministic, learnable Go admission policy stored in an additive SQLite table keyed by (sender_id, flow_kind) -> admit | ghost | skip. Project quarantined or amount-bearing skipped emails directly onto the main chronological transaction timeline (and a filterable Skipped view) as zero-balance-impact Timeline Ghost Rows pre-populated from raw_llm_json — with one-click [Promote] (zero LLM calls) and [Always Ignore] actions that flip that sender's policy cell and locally replay matching raw_emails without touching Gmail cursors.
Axis
Triage & Recovery Gate
Basis
direct: internal/llm/normalize.go:276-282 returns early on raw.EmailKind == "non_transaction" and discards all extracted fields; internal/syncrunner/runner.go:680-696 records an empty non_transaction row and increments skipped_count with no recovery UI; internal/llm/prompt.go:83-86 omits QRIS, bank transfers, and tips from card_charge/receipt. Live data/spender.db inspection proved all 5 false negatives (01b8eb35, 5ac46a86, eb26eaca, 9e83e37c, f9e81377) already hold valid amount_decimal + currency inside raw_llm_json, whereas the sole true negative (4f1ddad2) has amount_decimal: null. external: Rossum IDP contradiction-routed HITL queues + repo learnings (billing-statement-future-date-reconciliation-inflation.md, gmail-sync-sender-cursor-and-timestamp-null.md).
Rationale
Separating factual money-flow parsing from per-user accounting policy keeps the 7B model inside what it already does well, separates the 5 live false negatives from the 1 true negative with 100% precision/recall, and makes omissions unmistakable next to neighboring purchases (such as a Grab Tip sitting directly beneath its GrabFood order) instead of hiding them in a settings screen.
Downsides
Amount-bearing non-expenses (vouchers like 16006a26 and cancellations like fb13b866) briefly appear as Ghost Rows until ignored once per (sender_id, flow_kind); promoting historical emails must respect occurred_at_utc <= asOfUtc balance bounds; an additive policy table is required to avoid STRICT/CHECK migration churn on email_extractions.

Rejection Summary

All 5 Topic Axes have surviving candidates with zero coverage gaps. Ten consolidated candidates were cut or absorbed during adversarial basis verification and synthesis:

# Idea Reason Rejected
1 Sender role_hint Bypass & Edit UI (C03) Weak basis & below ambition floor: blindly bypassing non_transaction whenever role_hint != 'auto' breaks on Jenius cancellations (fb13b866) and billing statements (0b809370); exposing existing UpdateSender fields in Settings is routine plumbing.
2 Bogofilter Pre-LLM Triage Gate Trained on dismissed (C04) Refuted by verification: 1 of 4 dismissed rows (1e4bfdde) is a legitimate Jenius GRAB* charge deleted due to cancellation, which would poison Bayesian token weights against real charges; over-engineered for ~5 senders.
3 Unscoped 10-Class Schema & "First-Key Decoding" Hypothesis (C05 raw) Partially refuted and scoped into Idea #1: BuildExtractionJSONSchema (prompt.go:156-177) uses a Go map marshaled alphabetically so email_kind is 7th, not 1st, and a 10-way enum overtaxes Q4_K_M 7B; core policy decoupling survived in #1 with 5 tractable classes.
4 CAT Translation Memory & Settings Termbase (C07) Duplicates stronger ideas (#2, #3): output-side <Prefix>: <vendor> rewrites are solved cleanly by #2 and #3 without heavy localization jargon or an extra Termbase UI.
5 Runtime LLM-to-Deterministic-Parser Synthesis (C08) Too expensive relative to likely value: Qwen2.5-7B already extracts amounts and accounts accurately even on the 5 false negatives; generating and backtesting Go text parsers at runtime is brittle over-engineering for $0 local compute.
6 Idle "Sleep-Cycle" Playbook Curation & In-App Replay Harness (C09) Weak basis & scope drift: letting a 7B model curate prose playbook bullets conflicts with repo learnings that 7B models ignore prose instructions, and an idle eval daemon is developer tooling rather than product UX.
7 Original Living Category Profiles Citation & System-Prompt Mutation (C11 raw) Inaccurate citation corrected and folded into Idea #2: misattributed the DineOut: bug to resolveEnrichedRemark (enrichment.go:204) instead of findRemarkExamples (enrichment.go:155-157), and putting dynamic merchant lists in systemPrompt (prompt.go:93) would bust CachePrompt: true.
8 Standalone Two-Speed Memory Architecture (C12) Weak basis & duplicates #2/#3: deterministic merchant lookup cannot skip 7B parsing on raw email ingest (amounts and timestamps still require parsing); post-parse rule override is already covered by #3.
9 Full-Field Batch Magic Sweep via EnrichTransactionsFromSources (C14) Refuted codebase assumption & duplicates #4: EnrichTransactionsFromSources (enrichment.go:212-349) synthesizes emails of a single purchase into one preview, not batch multi-row sweeps; retroactive backlog cleanup is faster and safer via #4 Teach & Ripple.
10 Standalone Fellegi-Sunter Account Scorer & xxxx/**** Mask Claim (C16 raw) Partially refuted and folded into Idea #6: nonDigitsRe (accounts.go:100) already strips 5260xxxx55 and 5260****55 both to "526055", and a full Fellegi-Sunter model is overkill for 4 accounts; the verified cardStatus == "collision" bug (accounts_matching.go:64,71,97) + sender affinity were absorbed as the prerequisite half of #6.
